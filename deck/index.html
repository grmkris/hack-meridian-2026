Why Nod

Your AI can do the work. Paying is still awkward.

Constant interruptions

Every payment sends the task back to you.

A paid API result or a payment to a friend means leaving the conversation to finish the job yourself.

Too much access

A private key gives the AI access to your money.

You need a way to let it spend without trusting it with your whole balance.

Set what your AI can spend. It asks for your passkey when it needs more. Nod puts the wallet in your chat. Stellar enforces the spending permission on your account.

What we had to solve

Getting from a chat request to a payment.

The pocket model

Paying through existing x402 facilitators.

Hosted facilitators do not yet accept smart-account payers. Claude's key funds a small pocket under the account policy, in 1 USDC steps, then pays a compatible Stellar x402 seller.

your account→ policy →pocket→ stock x402 client →facilitator
Permission to spend

Your passkey sets the allowance.

You choose an amount and expiry when connecting the app. Your passkey installs its rule on the account, and you can revoke that app later.

Approval

You can still approve a larger payment.

Nod gives Claude an approval link. You see the recipient and amount on Nod's page, where your passkey signs the transaction.

Claim link

The recipient can start with a link.

Carol has a Stellar account but no USDC trustline. She accepts the link, and the trustline and payment settle together.

Market and ecosystem

Reach people through the AI they already use.

Connect from your AI app

Nod connects over MCP. We tested it in Claude and Goblin, and in ChatGPT through developer mode. It is not listed in the ChatGPT app store.

Buyers for Stellar's x402 sellers

Stellar builders have shipped paid APIs and paid MCP tools. They need buyers whose owners will let them spend.

A payment brings the recipient in

Recipients can open a link and create a passkey wallet on mainnet. Your AI can save contacts too. For a payment beyond the allowance, you approve a rule for that amount, valid seven days. Its key lives only in the link.

Live on mainnet

The full flow ran in claude.ai with my own wallet. Anyone can sign up and deposit Stellar USDC. Network fees in the mainnet run were 0.001 to 0.05 XLM per payment, paid by Nod.
