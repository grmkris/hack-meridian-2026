The problem

Your AI can do everything except pay safely.

Give it everything

Hand over a card or a private key.

No limit, no expiry, no way to see what it may spend. One bad prompt can empty the account.

Give it nothing

Make every payment yourself.

The task stops at checkout. A paid API call or paying a friend back waits for you.

There is no policy layer for x402 on Stellar today. Stellar developer docs, on agent payments

Innovation

Four ideas behind Nod.

The pocket model

A smart account pays any x402 seller today.

Hosted facilitators reject smart accounts. Claude's key fills its own small account under the policy, then pays.

your account→ policy →pocket→ stock x402 client →x402.org
Connect = allowance

Connecting an AI app installs its allowance onchain.

The OAuth consent page and the passkey signature are one step. One rule, expiry and revoke per app.

Ask, don't reject

An over-allowance payment becomes a passkey approval.

Most agent wallets just block it. Nod sends Claude a link, and your nod covers that exact recipient and amount.

Claim link

Pays people who have never held USDC.

Carol signs once. The USDC contract's trust function and the transfer run in one invocation.

Market and ecosystem

Every AI app is a door.

Distribution through MCP

Any MCP app can connect with one URL. Claude today, more hosts as their payment policies allow.

Buyers for Stellar's x402 sellers

Stellar builders have shipped paid APIs and paid MCP tools. They need buyers whose owners will let them spend.

People first, then teams

One allowance per AI app today. Next, a company card for every agent: limits, approvals and receipts.

Ready for a mainnet pilot

Every contract Nod uses is already on mainnet with identical WASM, except our 920-byte router. A full allowance lifecycle costs about 0.6 XLM at mainnet rent.
